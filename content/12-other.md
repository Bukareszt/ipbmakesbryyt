**Risks and mitigation** (semester affected).
- *Rejection at a top venue* (4–7): acceptance at these conferences is selective, so every paper has a
  resubmission path inside the 200-point set, to the next deadline 2–4 months later (e.g. NeurIPS → ICLR or
  CVPR → ICML → ECCV/ICCV), after revision using the reviews. With three core papers and several yearly
  deadlines, the degree requirement does not depend on one decision.
- *Changing venue points* (sem. 3–4): the new list may change points; venues are re-checked when it appears,
  and the plan keeps several 200-point alternatives per paper.
- *No robot access or sensor* (3–5): the hypotheses are decided on public benchmarks and datasets; the
  robot only validates, so validation can move by a semester or to another PWr robot.
- *Poor reconstruction of low-texture or large scenes* (3–4): depth priors, scene splitting, other scenes
  from the public dataset.
- *Sim-to-real predictivity fails* (4–5): pre-specified SRCC protocol; a negative result still answers RQ4.

**Prior work (background only).** A pre-PhD NLP paper (ACL 2025 Student Research Workshop) gives
experience in representation learning; it is not part of the dissertation.

**Ethics and data.** Own captures avoid or anonymize personal data (GDPR, PWr rules); public datasets are
used under their licences.
