# §8 Wkład w rozwój dyscypliny / Contribution to the discipline (max 1 page)

The expected results contribute to **information and communication technology**, specifically to machine
learning for robotics and autonomous systems:

1. **Method:** a complete, reproducible real-to-sim-to-real pipeline for mobile navigation. It turns
   short real captures into interactive, photorealistic training environments with collision geometry
   (RQ1, H1).
2. **Empirical knowledge:** the first systematic characterization of the **real-data budget vs.
   navigation performance** trade-off, answering "how much real data is enough" (RQ2, H4).
3. **Generalization technique:** a principled scheme for augmenting reconstructed scenes, so that
   policies go beyond the captured environments. This connects the digital-twin and domain-randomization
   lines of research (RQ3, H2).
4. **Closed-loop simulation correction:** a method to update simulation parameters from a few real
   rollouts, plus evidence that reconstructed simulation better predicts real performance (RQ4, H3).
5. **Open resources:** released code, reconstructed environments and a real-world evaluation dataset.
   These support reproducible sim-to-real benchmarking.

Together these lower the cost and time of deploying learning-based robots in new environments. That is a
key barrier to the practical adoption of embodied AI.
