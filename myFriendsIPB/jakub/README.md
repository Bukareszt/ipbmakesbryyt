## Topic

**PL:** Metoda oceny gotowości przodka do wejścia załogi po robotach strzałowych
na podstawie pomiarów realizowanych przez autonomicznego robota kroczącego

**EN:** A method for deciding whether a mine heading is ready for the crew to
enter after blasting, based on measurements taken by an autonomous legged robot.

## In short

- After blasting, someone has to check the heading for gases and loose rock
  before the crew goes in. This is one of the most dangerous jobs in the mine.
- Today the decision relies mostly on a fixed waiting time, experience and
  handheld detectors.
- Jakub's idea: a legged robot goes in first. It measures the air (including
  right at the muck pile, where trapped gases escape) and checks the roof and
  walls without touching them (thermal camera + lidar). From that data it
  gives a "ready / not ready" decision.
- Setting: KGHM copper mines (group blasting, H₂S hazard).

## Where we could connect

Places where a research partnership could make sense:

- autonomous / mobile robots in hazardous environments,
- gas sensing from mobile platforms (drone vs. legged robot),
- thermal imaging and lidar for detecting rock and structural hazards,
- turning sensor data into safety decisions,
- industrial partner: KGHM.
