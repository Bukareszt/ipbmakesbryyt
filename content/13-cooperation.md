# §13 Forma współpracy z promotorem / Planned cooperation with the supervisor

My supervisor and I plan weekly meetings, in which I present my progress and recent experimental results,
we agree on the next steps and we work on joint papers. In addition, I will attend the weekly meetings of
the supervisor's group of PhD students, where current results, problems and new work in the field are
discussed. Day-to-day contact will be by email and instant messaging, and code, trained models and the
LaTeX sources of papers will be kept in shared Git repositories.
