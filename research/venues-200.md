**Resubmission path** (rejection at a top venue): move the revised paper to the next 200-pt deadline, typically
2–4 months later: NeurIPS (May) → ICLR (Sep) → CVPR (Nov) → ICML (Jan) → ECCV/ICCV (Mar). Caveat: the
NeurIPS decision and the ICLR deadline are almost simultaneous (NeurIPS 2026 notification 24 Sep 2026, ICLR
2027 deadline 25 Sep 2026), so after a NeurIPS rejection the ICLR slot is usable only if the revision is ready
in days; the reliable next slots are **CVPR (~Nov) and ICML (~Jan)**. For P1 this still gives at least three
more 200-pt attempts (CVPR 2028, ICML 2028, ECCV 2028) before the end of sem. 6, well before the dissertation
deadline (30.09.2029).

**Timing for art. 186.** A conference paper counts when its proceedings are published; for NeurIPS this is
around or after the December meeting (NeurIPS 2027 → proceedings ~Dec 2027 – early 2028). Exact proceedings
publication dates: UNVERIFIED.
