# myFriendsIPB

Individual Research Plans (IPB) and topic reports from friends and colleagues.

**These are references, not templates.** Nothing here is meant to be copied into
my own IPB. Each folder records what a friend is working on so I can:

- see where our topics overlap or complement each other,
- cite or point to their work when it's relevant,
- find a possible research partner ("hey, I'd have a partner for this research").

Each friend has their own folder with:

- `README.md` – who they are, their topic in a few lines, and where our research
  could connect,
- the source document (converted to Markdown) as they shared it.
